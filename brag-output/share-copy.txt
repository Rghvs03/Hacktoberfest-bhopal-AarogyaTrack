## LinkedIn:
Delighted to unveil our Hacktoberfest Bhopal project: **AarogyaTrack — Every Visit Matters**.

In rural healthcare camps, travelling doctors face zero cell signal and scattered paper slips, leading to broken continuity of care.

We engineered a resilient, offline-first clinical Progressive Web App (PWA) powered by:
1. **Local-First Architecture:** Instant consult recording in local IndexedDB (Dexie.js), client-side UUIDs, and idempotent background sync to Neon PostgreSQL.
2. **Smart Rx Writepad:** Realistic clinician writepad supporting natural dictation (Web Speech API) with live clinical extraction into structured assessment & plan.
3. **Printable Rx Slips:** Standalone A5 prescription generation ready for paper dispensing without any internet.
4. **Longitudinal Trajectory Intelligence:** Google Gemini 1.5 synthesizes 6-month blood pressure progression and detects missing screening intervals.
5. **Hallmark Design System:** Clean, accessible "Specimen" theme with zero layout shift and clinical skeleton screens.
